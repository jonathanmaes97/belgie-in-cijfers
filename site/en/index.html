    What is this?

    The National Bank's barometer summarises the monthly business surveys (industry, construction, trade and services). It is mainly used to see the direction: if it rises, businesses are more positive.

    Why you can trust these figures

    1. Official sourcesEvery figure comes from an official institution, such as Eurostat, the National Bank or the Federal Planning Bureau. Each figure shows its source, with a link.
    2. Updated every dayA program fetches the figures automatically every day. Nobody types them in, and no AI is involved in the figures themselves.
    3. Anyone can checkAll data and calculations are public. You can download every figure and check it yourself.
