State of the Union / Health

How healthy are we?

How long and how healthily we live depends on many things: care, lifestyle, work and income. Here you can see how long people live on average and how much we spend together on healthcare.

Who decides?

Health insurance and hospital funding are largely federal. Prevention, elderly care and primary care are mainly the responsibility of the communities.

On this page

  • How old do we live to on average?
  • How much do we spend on healthcare?

A government is never the only cause of how a figure develops. Many figures only respond to policy years later, and they also depend on the world economy, European rules and the policies of the regions and communities.

Difficult words explained

GDP
Gross domestic product: the value of everything a country produces in a year. It is the usual measure of the size of an economy.
As a percentage of GDP
An amount compared with the size of the economy. This lets you compare countries of different sizes fairly.
