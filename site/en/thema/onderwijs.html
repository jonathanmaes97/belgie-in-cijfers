State of the Union / Education

How well educated are we?

A qualification increases the chance of a job and a good income. Here you can see how many young people leave school without a qualification, how many young adults have a higher education degree, and how many 15-year-olds fall short of the basic level in maths and reading in the international PISA test.

Who decides?

In Belgium, education is the responsibility of the communities: the Flemish, French and German-speaking Communities each have their own education system.

Higher education degree

How many young adults have a higher education degree?

52.7%

Belgium, 2025

  • That is higher than the EU average (44.8%).
  • In 2015 it was 43.1%.

This concerns people aged 25 to 34 with a bachelor's, master's or equivalent degree.

Early school leavers

How many young people leave school without a qualification?

7.3%

Belgium, 2025

  • That is lower than the EU average (9.1%).
  • In 2015 it was 10.1%.

Young people without a secondary school qualification often have a harder time on the labour market.

Low achievers in maths (PISA)

How many 15-year-olds fall short of the basic level in maths?

28.4%

Belgium, 2025

  • That is lower than the EU average (33.9%).
  • In 2015 it was 20.1%.

PISA is an international test taken by 15-year-olds every few years. Those below level 2 struggle with simple applications, such as comparing prices or reading a simple chart. Education is the responsibility of the communities; Eurostat only publishes the figure for Belgium as a whole.

Low achievers in reading (PISA)

How many 15-year-olds fall short of the basic level in reading?

28.9%

Belgium, 2025

  • That is lower than the EU average (31.3%).
  • In 2015 it was 19.5%.

Those below level 2 find it hard to identify the main idea of an ordinary text. As with maths, this covers Belgium as a whole; results per community appear in separate national reports.
