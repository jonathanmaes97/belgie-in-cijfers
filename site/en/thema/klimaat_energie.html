State of the Union / Climate and energy

How green is our energy?

Greenhouse gases are warming the planet. Europe wants emissions to fall and more energy to come from the sun, wind and other renewable sources. Here you can see how Belgium is doing: how much we emit, how much energy is renewable, what share of our electricity comes from nuclear power stations, and what electricity and gas cost for a household.

Who decides?

Responsibilities are shared: the federal government is responsible for offshore wind and nuclear energy, among other things, and the regions for onshore renewable energy and energy saving.

Renewable energy

How much of our energy comes from renewable sources?

14.9%

Belgium, 2025 provisional

  • That is lower than the EU average (26.2%).
  • In 2015 it was 8.1%.

This covers all energy: electricity, but also heating and transport. Sun, wind, biomass and hydropower count as renewable.

Emissions per inhabitant

How much greenhouse gas do we emit per person?

8.2 t

Belgium, 2024

  • In 2014 it was 10.1 t.

The emissions of the whole country (transport, industry, heating, agriculture) divided by the number of inhabitants. Forests that absorb CO₂ are deducted.

Nuclear power in our electricity

How much of our electricity comes from nuclear power stations?

40.5%

Belgium, 2024

  • That is higher than the EU average (23.0%).
  • In 2014 it was 46.5%.

This concerns electricity only, not all energy. Nuclear power stations emit virtually no greenhouse gases while running, but nuclear power does not count as renewable energy. Electricity imported from abroad is not included in this figure.

Electricity price

What does a kilowatt hour of electricity cost a household?

35.8c/kWh

Belgium, 2026 year not yet complete

  • That is higher than the EU average (28.3c/kWh).
  • In 2016 it was 26.4c/kWh.

An average household uses a few thousand kilowatt hours a year. The price includes the energy itself, network costs, taxes and VAT. Your own contract may be considerably more or less expensive.

Definition and source
Definition
Average price for households using 2,500 to 4,999 kWh per year, including all taxes and levies. Eurostat publishes two figures per year (per half-year); this is the average of both.
Calculation
prijs * 100 using stroomprijs_eur

Natural gas price

What does a kilowatt hour of gas cost a household?

8.5c/kWh

Belgium, 2026 year not yet complete

  • That is lower than the EU average (11.0c/kWh).
  • In 2016 it was 5.4c/kWh.

This consumption fits a household that heats with gas. Gas is cheaper per kilowatt hour than electricity, but you need much more of it for heating. Your own contract may differ considerably.

Definition and source
Definition
Average price for households using 20 to 199 gigajoules per year, including all taxes and levies. Eurostat publishes two figures per year (per half-year); this is the average of both.
Calculation
prijs * 100 using gasprijs_eur

What does a litre of heating oil cost?

Heating oil price

€1.541/l

26 October 2026

  • −€0.004/l compared with 19 October 2026
  • a year earlier: €0.805/l
  • EU: €1.760/l
What is this?

The average price of heating gas oil, including all taxes, as Belgium reports it to the European Commission every week. The Commission gives the price per 1,000 litres; we divide by 1,000. What you pay also depends on the quantity and your supplier.

Source: European Commission (Weekly Oil Bulletin) · raw data

What does a litre of petrol cost?

Maximum petrol price

€1.988/l

maximum price from 31 October 2026

  • average in September 2026: €2.057/l
What is this?

The official maximum price of 95 RON E10 petrol at the pump, including VAT, as set by the FPS Economy. Filling stations may not charge more, but they may charge less. The large figure is the price that applies now; the chart shows the monthly average.

Source: FPS Economy (maximum prices) · raw data

Difficult words explained

CO₂ equivalent
All greenhouse gases converted into the amount of CO₂ that would cause the same warming.
Gigawatt hour (GWh)
A measure of an amount of electricity: one million kilowatt hours.
